Your forearm muscles drive the glove

Hold Open or Close. The software reads the armband’s 8 channels in 150 ms windows and moves the glove after two confident windows in a row. Let go and it holds.

Open the hand first

Opening is usually the harder move after a stroke, so the open signal here is weaker than the close signal.

Put it back on the next day

The armband never lands in the same spot twice. Yesterday’s model sees a different signal, so a quick check updates it before the glove turns on.

The check is 5 seconds of rest and 5 reps of each move, about 30 seconds. Here it runs 3 times faster. It updates only the average signal for each move, then tests itself on those reps. Under 70% and the glove stays off.

When something goes wrong

Mixed signal

Tense the open and close muscles at once. No move reaches 0.80, so the glove holds still.

Power cut

The valves are open when unpowered, so the glove vents and lets go. It comes back idle.

Stuck valve

Pressure keeps climbing. Past the 60 kPa limit for 200 ms, the software vents the glove and stops.
